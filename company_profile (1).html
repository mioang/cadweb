About Us
Who We Are
Built on precision.
Driven by integrity.

PT Cipta Ananta Dharma Engineering Services is a structural engineering consultancy based in Jakarta, Indonesia. We provide end-to-end structural design and analysis services to property developers, architects, and contractors across residential, commercial, and mixed-use projects.

Founded on the belief that engineering excellence and professional service are inseparable, we combine rigorous technical analysis with transparency — ensuring every client understands the engineering behind their project.

Our Vision
To be the most trusted structural engineering partner for developers,contractors and architects in Indonesia — recognised for precision, reliability, and technical depth.
Our Mission
To deliver structurally sound, code-compliant, and cost-effective engineering solutions that give our clients confidence and assurance at every stage of their project.
The Story Behind Our Mark
Our logo encodes three layers of meaning: the Dharmachakra's 8 spokes represent our pathways of service; the belt-outrigger structural system reflects our core technical competency; the square are halved every step but half will never reach zero in infinite series.This represents our limitless potential.

Expertise & Standards
Our Technical Foundation
Depth of expertise.
Rigour at every level.

Our work is grounded in advanced structural engineering practice — combining deep technical knowledge with industry-leading software and full compliance with Indonesian and international design standards.

Design Standards
SNI
Standar Nasional Indonesia
Indonesia's national framework for structural design — covering concrete, steel, loading, and seismic requirements for all buildings constructed in Indonesia.
AISC
American Institute of Steel Construction
The internationally recognised standard for structural steel design and construction — widely adopted across Asia and the Americas for its rigour and clarity.
ACI
American Concrete Institute
The global benchmark for reinforced and prestressed concrete design — governing specification, detailing, and analysis of concrete structures worldwide.
International Standards
Eurocode · British Standard · and more
We also work within Eurocode (EN) and British Standard (BS) frameworks — supporting international clients and projects that require multi-standard compliance.
Software & Tools
Structural Analysis Software
Industry-leading finite element modelling tools for building analysis, seismic assessment, load combinations, and member design verification.
Python & Automation
Custom calculation scripts and automated PDF reporting tools — ensuring every output is traceable, repeatable, and professionally formatted.
